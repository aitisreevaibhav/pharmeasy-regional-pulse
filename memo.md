Guntur Sales Change – April to May 2026 [HIGH]

This is an increase of 122.19% from April to May. [HIGH]

## Key Insight

The main change seen in the data is the large increase in Guntur sales in May. [HIGH]

Sales increased by 122.19% from April to May. [HIGH]

But in June, sales went down by 28.11% compared with May. [HIGH]

This shows that the May increase was not continued in June. [MEDIUM]

## Evidence

- April sales: INR 62,442.27 [HIGH]
- May sales: INR 138,738.93 [HIGH]

- April to May change: +122.19% [HIGH]

The project uses an 8% change as the alert level. [LOW]

Both changes for Guntur are above this level. [HIGH]

This can help understand what caused the large change in May. [MEDIUM]
